OUR STORY

How We Started

Emmynet ICT Hub began with a simple observation: too many businesses were stuck waiting on tech talent that didn't exist yet and too many aspiring developers were stuck learning theory with no path to real work. We decided to close that gap ourselves.

Today, we operate as both a development studio and a training institution, feeding one side with the other. Our client projects give trainees real experience. Our trainees become some of our best hires and collaborators. It's a model built for growth theirs and ours.

▦Founded in 2026 in Benue, Nigeria
